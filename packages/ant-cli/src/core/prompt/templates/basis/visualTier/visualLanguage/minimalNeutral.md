## Visual Language: Minimal Neutral

Use a minimal neutral visual language.

**Tone**: Quiet, calm, understated, deliberate.
**Typography**: Light weight, restrained hierarchy, generous line height.
**Contrast**: Low — quiet separation, avoid harsh boundaries.
**Radius**: Restrained — not zero, but barely noticeable.
**Color posture**: Near-monochrome. Very restrained accent usage. Let content speak.

Constraint: Keep the interface understated and deliberate.
Constraint: Do NOT add emphasis unless absolutely necessary. Quiet is the default.
