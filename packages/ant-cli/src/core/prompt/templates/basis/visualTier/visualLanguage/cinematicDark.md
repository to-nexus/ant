## Visual Language: Cinematic Dark
supportedModes: dark

### Identity
Immersive, dramatic, and full-bleed. Recognizable by its vast dark canvas, extreme whitespace, and the feeling that content is floating in a theater of negative space. The impression is cinematic gravity — every element is a scene.

### Typography
--font-heading: "Sora"
--font-body: "Sora"
--font-mono: "JetBrains Mono"
Light to regular heading weight — the drama comes from size and space, not bold weight. Generous letter-spacing on headings. Clean geometric body at regular weight with elevated line-height.

### Signature
Theatrical composition — content floats in vast dark canvas, framed by negative space rather than visible dividers.
Near-white primary on near-black canvas creating a stark yet soft photographic contrast.
Full-bleed hero sections that treat images and media as primary compositional elements.

### Constraints
Constraint: Do NOT fill the canvas — leave generous emptiness; the void is intentional.
Constraint: Do NOT use heavy borders or visible dividers — separation comes from space alone.
Constraint: Do NOT introduce bright or saturated accent colors — the palette stays monochromatic with a subtle cool tint.
Constraint: Do NOT add playful or decorative elements — maintain the serious cinematic tone.
