## Visual Language: Clean Bright
supportedModes: light

### Identity
Bright, airy, and optimistic. Recognizable by its pure white canvas, vivid blue primary, and generous use of light. The impression is clarity and approachability — a modern SaaS product that feels effortless to use.

### Typography
--font-heading: "Inter"
--font-body: "Inter"
--font-mono: "JetBrains Mono"
Medium weights for body, semibold for headings. Clear size steps with a restrained modular scale. Sans-serif throughout — functional, not decorative.

### Signature
Crisp white card surfaces with very faint borders and no visible shadow.
Single vivid blue accent that carries all primary emphasis across the interface.
Light tinted backgrounds (blue-wash) for selected or active states.

### Constraints
Constraint: Do NOT introduce dark surfaces or dark cards in this light-only palette.
Constraint: Do NOT add gradients, glows, or decorative overlays.
Constraint: Do NOT mix multiple hue families — blue is the sole accent hue.
Constraint: Do NOT use ornamental illustrations or decorative elements unless explicitly required.
