## Visual Language: Fintech Premium

Use a fintech premium visual language.

**Tone**: Precise, refined, deliberate, high-trust.
**Typography**: Precise sizing, refined spacing, premium feel without being flashy.
**Contrast**: Refined — subtle differentiation, not harsh separation.
**Radius**: Restrained — slightly softer than enterprise, but controlled.
**Color posture**: Muted, intentional palette. Premium does not mean colorful.

Constraint: Keep the UI elegant and controlled.
Constraint: Do NOT use flashy gradients, bright accents, or playful elements.
