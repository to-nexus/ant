## Visual Language: Enterprise

Use an enterprise visual language.

**Tone**: Stable, trustworthy, efficient, restrained.
**Typography**: High legibility, strong hierarchy, low ornamentation.
**Contrast**: Clear and direct — prioritize readability over aesthetics.
**Radius**: Minimal — lean toward angular, controlled softening only where functional.
**Color posture**: Conservative palette. Avoid trendy colors. Favor neutrals with a single functional accent.

Constraint: Favor clarity, trust, and efficiency over novelty.
Constraint: Do NOT add decorative elements. Every visual choice must serve function.
