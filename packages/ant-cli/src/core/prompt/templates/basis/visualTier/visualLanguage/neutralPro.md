## Visual Language: Neutral Pro
supportedModes: both

### Identity
Restrained, mature, and trustworthy. Recognizable by its desaturated palette, sharp typographic hierarchy, and absence of decorative color. The impression is corporate competence — the interface fades behind the work.

### Typography
--font-heading: "Inter"
--font-body: "Inter"
--font-mono: "JetBrains Mono"
Semibold headings, regular body. Tight tracking on headings for a professional feel. Restrained type scale — hierarchy through weight and size, never through color or decoration.

### Signature
Near-monochrome palette where primary is a deep slate rather than a vivid hue.
Subtle border-and-background differentiation without relying on shadow or depth cues.
Information density prioritized over visual embellishment.

### Constraints
Constraint: Do NOT introduce vivid or saturated colors — all hues must remain muted and desaturated.
Constraint: Do NOT add gradients, glows, or animated decorative elements.
Constraint: Do NOT rely on color alone for hierarchy — use weight, size, and spacing.
Constraint: Do NOT add rounded pill shapes — keep radius controlled and small.
