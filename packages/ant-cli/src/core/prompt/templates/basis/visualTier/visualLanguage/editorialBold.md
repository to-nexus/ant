## Visual Language: Editorial Bold
supportedModes: light

### Identity
Dramatic, typographic, and magazine-inspired. Recognizable by its oversized serif headlines, high black-on-white contrast, and generous editorial spacing. The impression is bold authority — every element is placed with intentional weight.

### Typography
--font-heading: "Instrument Serif"
--font-body: "Inter"
--font-mono: "JetBrains Mono"
Oversized serif headings at bold weight — headlines dominate the page. Sans-serif body at regular weight for clean readability. Strong contrast between heading and body size to create editorial rhythm.

### Signature
Near-black primary used for headlines and CTA surfaces, creating a high-contrast typographic layout.
A single warm accent (red-orange) reserved for editorial highlights and emphasis marks.
Generous vertical whitespace between sections that frames content like a magazine spread.

### Constraints
Constraint: Do NOT soften headings with light weights or small sizes — maintain editorial scale.
Constraint: Do NOT introduce multiple accent hues — the warm accent is singular and deliberate.
Constraint: Do NOT add rounded pill shapes or playful geometry — keep edges sharp and editorial.
Constraint: Do NOT use gradients or decorative illustrations.
