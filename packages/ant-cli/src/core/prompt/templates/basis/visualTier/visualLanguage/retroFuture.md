## Visual Language: Retro Future
supportedModes: both

### Identity
Nostalgic, optimistic, and glossy. Recognizable by its pastel neon palette, rounded geometric shapes, and the feeling of a 1960s–1980s vision of the future. The impression is retro-futuristic fun — chrome dashboards and candy-colored control panels.

### Typography
--font-heading: "Varela Round"
--font-body: "Varela Round"
--font-mono: "JetBrains Mono"
Rounded sans-serif throughout for a friendly, retro feel. Semibold headings, regular body. The soft letterforms reinforce the optimistic, space-age character.

### Signature
Pastel neon dual-tone: teal-cyan primary and bubblegum pink accent evoking retro-futuristic color science.
Glossy, polished appearance — subtle highlight gradients on cards that suggest plastic or chrome finishes.
Large rounded corners and pill-shaped elements that recall vintage control panels and instrument dials.

### Constraints
Constraint: Do NOT use dark or muted tones as primary colors — the palette is pastel and bright.
Constraint: Do NOT use sharp corners or brutalist geometry — all forms must feel soft and rounded.
Constraint: Do NOT add realistic textures or photographs as backgrounds — maintain the synthetic retro feel.
Constraint: Do NOT use serif fonts — rounded geometric sans-serif is essential to the retro character.
