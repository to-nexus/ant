## Interaction Grammar: Calm Premium

Use calm premium interaction.

**Motion**: Soft transitions (200-300ms). Gentle ease-in-out. No aggressive movement.
**Hover**: Very subtle — faint background shift or slight opacity change.
**Focus**: Elegant focus ring. Refined color.
**Active**: Quiet press state. Minimal visual displacement.
**Loading**: Refined skeleton or subtle pulse. No spinning icons.
**Empty**: Clean, understated empty state. Minimal decoration.
**Error**: Quiet error messaging. Inline, calm color.

Constraint: Keep interaction elegant and understated.
Constraint: Do NOT use bounce, aggressive scale, or flashy transitions.
