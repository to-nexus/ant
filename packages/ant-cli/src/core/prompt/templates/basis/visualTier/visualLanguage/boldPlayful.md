## Visual Language: Bold Playful
supportedModes: both

### Identity
Energetic, cheerful, and unapologetically vivid. Recognizable by its saturated primary palette, generous rounded corners, and the feeling that the interface is having fun. The impression is friendly confidence — bold choices that make users smile.

### Typography
--font-heading: "Nunito"
--font-body: "Nunito"
--font-mono: "JetBrains Mono"
Bold headings with extra-bold weight for impact. Rounded letterforms that reinforce the playful personality. Size jumps are generous — headings should feel big and confident.

### Signature
Saturated purple primary paired with a vivid green accent for dual-tone energy.
Generously rounded corners on all interactive elements and containers.
Bold, chunky typography that feels approachable and fun rather than corporate.

### Constraints
Constraint: Do NOT desaturate the palette — vibrancy is intentional, not accidental.
Constraint: Do NOT use thin or light font weights — maintain bold, confident typography.
Constraint: Do NOT add corporate or formal styling — the tone must stay playful.
Constraint: Do NOT use more than two saturated hue families (primary + accent).
