## Visual Language: Cyberpunk Neon
supportedModes: dark

### Identity
Electric, angular, and futuristic. Recognizable by its near-black void, vivid cyan-magenta neon pairing, and sharp geometric edges. The impression is digital intensity — a high-tech terminal glowing in the dark.

### Typography
--font-heading: "Orbitron"
--font-body: "Inter"
--font-mono: "JetBrains Mono"
Geometric, angular heading face for a futuristic tone. Clean sans-serif body for legibility against dark backgrounds. Uppercase headings with wide letter-spacing for a sci-fi display feel.

### Signature
Neon cyan primary and hot magenta accent that create a classic cyberpunk dual-tone glow.
Subtle neon glow halos on primary interactive elements and highlighted surfaces.
Angular, nearly-sharp corners that reinforce the hard-edged technological aesthetic.

### Constraints
Constraint: Do NOT use warm or organic tones — the palette is strictly cold neon.
Constraint: Do NOT soften corners into large radii — keep geometry sharp and angular.
Constraint: Do NOT introduce light-mode surfaces or bright backgrounds.
Constraint: Do NOT overuse glow effects — reserve them for primary focus elements only.
Constraint: Do NOT use serif or decorative fonts — maintain the technical display aesthetic.
