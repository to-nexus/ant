## Surface System: Tinted

Use lightly tinted surfaces for subtle tone and depth.

**Panel treatment**: Very light background color shifts to distinguish layers.
**Depth**: Color-assisted — tint replaces shadow for layer separation.
**Borders**: Optional. Tint difference can replace border in many cases.
**Atmosphere**: Subtle tone layering. Mild brand or functional color presence.

Constraint: Keep tint restrained and consistent across all surfaces.
Constraint: Do NOT over-tint. Maximum 2-3 tint levels. Readability is paramount.
