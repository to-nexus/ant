## Visual Language: Warm Natural
supportedModes: light

### Identity
Warm, organic, and grounded. Recognizable by its cream-tinted canvas, earthy accent tones, and the pairing of a serif heading face with a clean sans-serif body. The impression is comfortable craftsmanship — inviting and human.

### Typography
--font-heading: "Source Serif 4"
--font-body: "Inter"
--font-mono: "JetBrains Mono"
Serif headings at medium-bold weight for an editorial warmth. Sans-serif body for readability. Generous line-height to let text breathe.

### Signature
Cream-tinted backgrounds that give the interface a paper-like warmth instead of clinical white.
Serif–sans-serif pairing that signals thoughtfulness and editorial care.
Earthy amber and brown tones replacing the typical cold blue SaaS palette.

### Constraints
Constraint: Do NOT use pure white (#fff / oklch 1.0) as background — maintain the warm tint.
Constraint: Do NOT introduce cool-toned blues or purples — keep the palette within warm hues.
Constraint: Do NOT add dark surfaces or dark cards in this light-only palette.
Constraint: Do NOT use decorative gradients or synthetic textures.
