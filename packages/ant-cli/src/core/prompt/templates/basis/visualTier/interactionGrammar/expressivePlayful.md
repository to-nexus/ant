## Interaction Grammar: Expressive Playful

### Micro-interaction
**Hover**: Energetic — bounce or spring scale-up, playful shadow pop, color shift.
**Focus**: Bold, visible focus ring with brand color.
**Active**: Snappy press with spring-back (scale 0.95→1 with overshoot).
**Loading**: Animated skeleton with personality. Playful spinner or bouncing dots.
**Empty**: Illustrated empty state with cheerful tone and clear CTA.
**Error**: Friendly error state. Warm color, encouraging copy.

### Presentation Motion
**Page entrance**: Staggered pop-in from bottom. Elements arrive in rhythmic sequence.
**Section reveal**: Scale-based entrance on scroll (scale 0.9→1 + fade, spring easing).
**Parallax**: Light parallax on decorative elements only. Content stays grounded.
**Hero**: Bouncy entrance with staggered children (spring easing, 100-120ms stagger).
**Duration**: 200-350ms per element. Spring easing preferred. Stagger 80-120ms between siblings.

Constraint: All motion MUST respect prefers-reduced-motion.
Constraint: Do NOT animate text content — only containers, icons, and media.
Constraint: Playfulness must not compromise usability or readability.
