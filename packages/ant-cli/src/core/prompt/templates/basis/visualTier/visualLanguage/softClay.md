## Visual Language: Soft Clay
supportedModes: light

### Identity
Puffy, tactile, and pastel-drenched. Recognizable by its rounded 3D-extrusion effect, soft colored shadows, and candy-like palette. The impression is playful physicality — as if the interface were molded from soft clay.

### Typography
--font-heading: "Poppins"
--font-body: "Poppins"
--font-mono: "JetBrains Mono"
Semibold headings with rounded, geometric letterforms. Medium body weight. Comfortable line-height to match the airy, pillowy feel of the interface.

### Signature
Puffy 3D extrusion effect — containers appear to push outward with soft colored shadows matching their fill tone.
Pastel palette with a lavender primary and mint accent for a candy-like dual-tone pairing.
Extra-large border-radius on all containers creating a pillowed, inflated appearance.

### Constraints
Constraint: Do NOT use sharp corners or zero-radius elements — everything must feel rounded and soft.
Constraint: Do NOT use dark or high-contrast surfaces — the palette must remain pastel and light.
Constraint: Do NOT use hard black shadows — shadows must be colored and soft to maintain the clay effect.
Constraint: Do NOT introduce angular or geometric harshness — all shapes should feel organic and puffy.
