## Visual Language: Dark Luxury
supportedModes: dark

### Identity
Opulent, refined, and exclusive. Recognizable by its near-black canvas, warm gold accent, and the contrast of a decorative serif headline against clean body text. The impression is premium exclusivity — every detail signals quality and restraint.

### Typography
--font-heading: "Playfair Display"
--font-body: "Inter"
--font-mono: "JetBrains Mono"
Elegant serif headings at medium-bold weight for luxury presence. Clean sans-serif body for readability on dark backgrounds. Refined contrast in type scale — headings command attention, body stays quiet.

### Signature
Warm gold accent that carries brand emphasis across CTA, links, and highlights.
Near-black canvas with a subtle warm undertone (not cold gray) that feels rich rather than harsh.
Serif–sans-serif pairing that evokes premium print design and editorial quality.

### Constraints
Constraint: Do NOT use bright or neon colors — accent must remain warm gold family only.
Constraint: Do NOT introduce light-mode surfaces or bright white backgrounds.
Constraint: Do NOT overuse the gold accent — reserve it for primary emphasis moments.
Constraint: Do NOT add playful or rounded geometry — maintain sharp, composed forms.
