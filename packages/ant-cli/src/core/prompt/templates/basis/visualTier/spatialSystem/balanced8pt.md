## Spatial System: Balanced 8pt

Use a balanced spatial system with consistent 8pt-based rhythm.

**Base unit**: 8px. Scale: 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48.
**Density**: Moderate. Clear breathing room without waste.
**Section gaps**: Medium (24-32px between sections).
**Card/panel padding**: Standard (16-24px internal padding).
**Rhythm**: Repeatable and predictable across all screens.

Constraint: Keep spacing intentional and repeatable.
Constraint: Do NOT use spacing values outside the scale (no 10px, 15px, 20px, etc.).
Constraint: Every gap, padding, and margin must be traceable to this scale.
