## Surface System: Glass Light

Use restrained glass-like surfaces with light translucency.

**Panel treatment**: Very subtle blur and translucency on select surfaces.
**Depth**: Light frosted effect. Content behind is barely perceptible.
**Borders**: Thin, semi-transparent borders to define edges against translucent backgrounds.
**Readability**: Strong contrast safeguards. Text must remain fully legible.

Constraint: Keep the glass effect minimal and secondary to content clarity.
Constraint: Do NOT apply glass to dense data areas. Reserve for hero/overview surfaces only.
Constraint: Do NOT reduce text contrast for aesthetic purposes.
