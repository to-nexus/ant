## Surface System: Solid

Use solid surfaces with clear separation.

**Panel treatment**: Grounded, opaque backgrounds. Clear figure-ground distinction.
**Depth**: Minimal material effect. No blur, no translucency.
**Borders**: Explicit where needed for separation. Prefer border over shadow.
**Readability**: Direct and unambiguous surface boundaries.

Constraint: Keep surfaces readable and stable.
Constraint: Do NOT add shadow, blur, or tint effects.
