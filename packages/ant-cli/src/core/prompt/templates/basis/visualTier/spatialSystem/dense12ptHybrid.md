## Spatial System: Dense 12pt Hybrid

Use a hybrid dense spatial system with strong outer structure and compact inner blocks.

**Outer structure**: 12px base. Scale: 12, 24, 36, 48 for section-level spacing.
**Inner data zones**: 4px base. Scale: 4, 8, 12, 16 for within-card/panel spacing.
**Density**: High inside data blocks, structured outside.
**Section gaps**: Moderate outer (24-36px), compact inner (8-12px).

Constraint: Make the layout feel intentional rather than crowded.
Constraint: Do NOT apply inner compact spacing to section-level gaps.
Constraint: Outer rhythm and inner rhythm are independent systems.
