## Visual Language: Modern SaaS

Use a modern SaaS visual language.

**Tone**: Clean, polished, friendly, productized.
**Typography**: Medium weight, clear hierarchy, sans-serif dominant.
**Contrast**: Medium — enough to separate but not harsh.
**Radius**: Soft but controlled — not overly rounded, not sharp.
**Color posture**: Professional with restrained accent. Avoid decorative gradients.

Constraint: Prefer clarity and consistency over decorative styling.
Constraint: Do NOT add ornamental elements unless explicitly required.
