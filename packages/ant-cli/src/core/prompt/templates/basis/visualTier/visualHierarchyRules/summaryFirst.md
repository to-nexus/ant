## Visual Hierarchy: Summary First

Use summary-first hierarchy that emphasizes top-level status and KPIs.

**Primary**: Summary metrics, KPIs, and high-signal status indicators at the top.
**Secondary**: Detail tables, charts, and supporting data below the summary.
**Tertiary**: Controls, filters, and navigation are subordinate to data.

Constraint: Keep supporting information subordinate to the summary.
Constraint: Do NOT give detail views the same visual prominence as the summary.
Constraint: The first viewport should show the most important signal.
