## Art Concept: Hand-Drawn Storybook

**Activation gate**: `gameArtTier.concept === 'handDrawnStorybook'`.

An illustrated, hand-made direction — watercolor and ink on paper, warm and organic.

### Palette identity

Soft, slightly desaturated pigments with the tooth of paper showing through.

| Slot | Tone |
|---|---|
| Primary | A muted natural hue (sage / ochre / dusty blue) with washy variation. |
| Accent | A warmer saturated ink accent for focus. |
| Danger | An earthy red, still within the pigment family. |
| Background | Warm paper cream / off-white with subtle texture. |

Color has grain and edge bleed — pigment pools and dries unevenly, never a perfectly flat fill.

### Silhouette

- **Weight**: light-to-medium. Organic, imperfect contours with human wobble.
- **Complexity**: moderate — expressive linework carries character; interiors stay loose.
- **Edge style**: visible ink strokes with varying pressure; edges are drawn, not geometric.

### Lighting tone

- **Light source**: soft and diffuse, implied through wash density rather than a hard key.
- **Shadow policy**: watercolor shadow washes, layered translucency; no crisp cast shadows.
- **Atmospherics**: gentle — soft vignettes, paper grain, faint bleed at edges.

### Motion tone

- **Tempo**: gentle ease, slightly loose timing (a hint of hand-animated imperfection).
- **Scale**: soft — nothing snaps mechanically; slow organic drift on idle.

### Reference cluster (text references only)

- Watercolor picture books; ink-and-wash indie adventures; hand-animated storybook cutscenes.

### Outputs and code-time consequences

- **Minimal-guide caveat**: authentic watercolor/ink is a HANDOFF / DESIGN.md seed strength (real textures placed by the user). Greenfield code approximates the DIRECTION — soft washy gradients, grain overlays, hand-loose shapes — under the minimum-playable floor.
- Palette via soft multi-stop gradients + subtle noise/grain; SVG paths favor organic, slightly irregular curves.
- `2d` — a hand-drawn illustrated plane; a 3d camera breaks the paper conceit.

### HUD layout defaults (D28 — concept-derived)

- **Spacing rhythm**: `airy8pt` — relaxed, breathing layout.
- **Surface treatment**: `soft` on paper — panels read as torn/framed paper with a soft edge, minimal border.
- **Typography weight**: humanist serif or hand-lettered display for headings; warm readable body.
- **Border radius**: soft irregular 8–16px feel; avoid mechanical precision.
- **Focus ring / interaction tone**: a soft ink underline / gentle wash on focus; slow 250–400ms fades.
