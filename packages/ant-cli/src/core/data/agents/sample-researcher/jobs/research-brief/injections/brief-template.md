Section-by-section skeleton for a research brief.

Every brief uses this structure, in this order:

```markdown
# <Topic>

## Summary
Three to five sentences a reader can act on without reading further.

## Findings
The evidence, grouped by sub-question. Each claim carries a citation and a
confidence label (see the agent's citation rules).

## Sources
Every source used, one per line: linked title — one-line note on what it
contributed and how reliable it seemed.

## Open questions
What remains unverified or contested, and what would resolve it.
```

Sections may grow across turns, but none may be removed; an empty section
states why it is empty.
